Student travel, made easier.

Drivers post trips they're already taking. Passengers search for rides and request seats. Everyone is a verified university student.

HomeTern is outside my primary discipline, and that's the point of including it here. It shows how I approach an unfamiliar technical problem: learn what's needed, design the system, build it, and test it.

Why build it.

Students regularly travel between campus, airports and their hometowns, often at the same times as each other. A university-focused platform can make those trips more accessible and affordable, but only if people trust who they're riding with.

That makes trust and safety a core engineering requirement, not an add-on: verified identities, verified university emails, driver documentation and trip safety features.

Money

Payments between students routed through a payment platform, not handled directly.

Safety

Trip tracking and safety sessions while a ride is in progress.
